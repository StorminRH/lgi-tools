## Delivered outcome

LGI.tools now expresses interface, data, and display typography through semantic roles; declares
page hierarchy and responsive breakpoints through shared owners; and gives the header, content
rail, mobile contents drawer, page-settings menu, and bounded scroll regions consistent,
discoverable behavior across the application.

## Divergences from plan

- Plan `PD-3` specified removing per-cell navigation dividers. The operator found the undivided
  strip visually unclear and directed that visible separators be restored while the tabs remained
  right-aligned beside account state. Authority: operator direction.
- Persistent, higher-contrast scrollbar styling was not enumerated in the plan. The operator
  identified undiscoverable overflow in the content rail and planner popovers, so one shared
  bounded-scroll primitive now owns that affordance. Authority: operator direction.
- Plan `SC-8` named stale catalogue and planner example routes. Live 200-response evidence selected
  `/sites/3` and `/industry/29991` for the matrix instead. Authority: discovered runtime evidence.
- The pre-PR design review found that the generic drawer carried content-browser-specific hooks and
  the menu accepted broad native prop bags. The hooks were made consumer-neutral and the menu seam
  narrowed to data attributes before the final gate. Authority: required design review.

## Final surfaces

- `src/app/globals.css`, `src/components/ui/type-roles.ts`, and
  `src/components/ui/cn.ts` — own the semantic presentation tokens and their class-merging rules.
- `src/components/ui/page-shell.tsx` and `src/components/ui/page-head.tsx` — require an explicit
  page mode and own the shared title hierarchy.
- `src/components/ui/navigation-menu.tsx`, `src/components/ui/menu.tsx`, and
  `src/components/composition/AppHeaderShell.tsx` — own desktop and collapsed navigation
  presentation, alignment, and menu rows.
- `src/components/ui/content-browser.tsx`, `src/components/ui/content-browser-drawer.tsx`, and
  `src/components/ui/drawer.tsx` — own the layered desktop contents rail and mobile contents sheet.
- `src/components/ui/scroll-area.ts` — owns the persistent bounded-scroll affordance used by content
  rails, menus, tables, and planner popovers.
- `src/composition/ui-adoption-census.ts`, `src/composition/ui-adoption-registry.ts`, and
  `eslint.config.mjs` — reject retired presentation families, dead allowlist entries, legacy
  typography classes, and unowned Base UI imports.
- `scripts/ux-capture-args.mjs`, `scripts/ux-capture.mjs`, and `docs/ux-check/` — own the seven-width
  capture matrix, viewport contexts, reduced-motion support, and durable responsive probes.

## Successor notes

- Direct swipe-follow movement is deliberate even under reduced motion; only the drawer's animated
  settle transitions are suppressed.
- The content rail's active wash appears after hydration because the static fallback has no route
  slug. The operator reviewed and accepted that state change.
- Visible desktop navigation separators are an approved design decision, not an incomplete removal.
- The persistent scrollbar belongs on bounded regions through `scrollArea`; do not restyle each
  consumer independently.
