## Delivered outcome

Durable Neon map access now projects one-way into Convex as a minimal
`{ mapId, userId, roles }` claim set. Teardown and resynchronization are the
same full-state reconcile, account purge and identity mutations re-project or
tear down best-effort, and deleting a claim evicts an already-live gated
subscription with `FORBIDDEN`. Per-subscription read cost at pinned fixture
sizes is recorded in the scratchpad.

## Divergences from plan

- **Plan statement:** Extend `src/data/maps/purge.ts` to call composition
  projection seams directly after durable deletes.
  **Built instead:** `mapsPurgeContributor` remains in `src/data/maps/purge.ts`
  and composition registers projection hooks through
  `registerMapAccessProjectionPurgeHooks` (wired from
  `src/composition/map-access-purge.ts`).
  **Why:** Fallow forbids `data` → `composition` imports; the hook seam keeps
  table ownership co-located while still invoking `projectMapAccess`.
  **Authority:** Evidence: `.fallowrc.json` data-zone allow list and the online
  status / composition wiring precedent.

- **Plan statement:** OOS route-file protection and "adds no other export"
  beyond the plan-named projection surfaces; production triggers limited to the
  maps purge contributor (PD-2 / OOS-2).
  **Built instead:** `registerIdentityProjectionHooks` +
  `src/composition/map-access-identity.ts`, with calls from
  `admin-users.ts` / `account-purge.ts` / Better Auth `account.create` after
  hook, and side-effect registration on the identity API routes
  (`account/characters/unlink`, `admin/characters/unlink`,
  `admin/characters/reassign`, `account/purge-character`, `auth/[...all]`,
  plus purge `register-all`).
  **Why:** Admin/self-service unlink, reassign, absorb-on-proof, fresh
  character link, and emptied-account user deletes mutate Neon ownership
  without the maps purge contributor and left stale Convex claims until resync.
  **Authority:** Operator: directed the in-session fix, vetoed backlog
  deferral (confirmed 2026-07-31 in the fix directive).

## Final surfaces

- `src/composition/map-access-projection.ts` — compute + one-way project/teardown/
  user-purge door clients (`projectMapAccess`, `computeMapAccessClaims`, plus
  helpers `teardownMapAccessProjection`, `purgeUserMapAccessProjection`).
- `src/composition/map-access-purge.ts` — registers purge projection hooks.
- `src/composition/map-access-identity.ts` — identity-mutation projection hooks
  (unlink / reassign / absorb / emptied-user teardown).
- `src/platform/auth/identity-projection-hooks.ts` — Fallow seam for those hooks.
- `src/data/maps/queries.ts` — batched candidate-user lookups and purge capture
  helpers (`getUserIdsOwningCharacters`, `getUserIdsInCorporations`,
  `getMapIdsWithCorporationGrants`, `getMapIdsWithCharacterGrant`,
  `getOwnedMapIds`, `getCharacterCorporationId`).
- `src/data/maps/purge.ts` — maps purge contributor with optional projection
  hooks (`deleteCharacterMapGrants`, `deleteOwnedMaps`).
- `convex/mapAccessProjection.ts` — sole production `mapAccess` writer
  (`reconcileMapClaims`, `purgeUserClaims`).
- `convex/http.ts` — bearer-gated `/project-map-access` and `/purge-map-access`.

Helper exports beyond the two plan-named functions also include
`teardownMapAccessProjection`, `purgeUserMapAccessProjection`,
`deleteCharacterMapGrants`, `deleteOwnedMaps`, and the five capture/lookup
queries listed above.
