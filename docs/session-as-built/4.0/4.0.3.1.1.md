## Delivered outcome

A pure, deterministic graph-facts-to-positions layout kernel exists under
`src/mapper/layout/`. Both contract candidates ran an identical seeded property
harness (12 chains, 2–60 systems, loops/duplicates/orphan phases; growth
replays), and the operator selected the compass-sector engine at the G-1 gate —
the contract's designed-fallback approval — with the `fixed-slot` posture and
wide sibling fan (`siblingSpread: 3`) as the shipped default. The DC-5
comparison record: ELK radial (elkjs 0.12.0; full documented matrix — sorter
NONE/ID × wedgeCriteria NODE_SIZE/LEAF_NUMBER × compactor NONE/
RADIAL_COMPACTION/WEDGE_COMPACTION; `org.eclipse.elk.randomSeed` pinned because
the default seeds from system time) was deterministic in all 8 surviving cells
but failed the zero-overlap hard gate in every one (23–24 violating pairs per
corpus run, minimum pair distance 14 px) and hit a reproducible infinite loop in
all 4 WEDGE_COMPACTION cells (corpus seed 12, a 5-system chain, at nodeNode
spacing 100; the bundled build computes synchronously and cannot be interrupted
in-process), with heavy spawn movement (root-child add moved up to 37 nodes,
sector fill up to 58 with 56 outside the sibling group). The compass engine
measured: deterministic (byte-identical digests in- and cross-process), zero
overlap (minimum distance 147 px against the 140 px gate), zero spanning-tree
crossings over the corpus, zero attached-node movement across all replayed
spawns, ~0.8 ms per 60-system layout, zero dependencies. ELK was deleted whole
and `elkjs` uninstalled (net-zero manifest diff; the loser's code never reached
a commit, so this record is the sole surviving evidence). Citations:
eclipse.dev/elk radial option pages and the elkjs README (retrieved 2026-08-01
at planning; option ids re-verified against the installed 0.12.0 bundle and
`knownLayoutOptions()` at execution). Selection-time measurement artifacts
(scorecard, per-cell JSON, 54 SVG snapshots, spawn animations) were reviewed by
the operator and preserved outside the repository at
`../atlas/` (operator's copy). The kernel is not wired into the live map:
`gridAssigner` remains the production assigner and the 4.0.2.3 chain/canvas
production files are byte-unchanged.

## Divergences from plan

- **Plan statement:** "Interfaces and contracts" pinned `deriveChainTree` as a
  full-pass rescan ("the scan repeats in full passes until a pass makes no new
  attachment").
  **Built instead:** an edge-incremental replay — connections processed one at
  a time in creation order, with earlier blocked connections drained to a
  fixpoint after every attachment. Both plan-pinned classification examples
  (single-pass diamond; multi-pass `(B,C)`-then-`(A,B)`) hold unchanged.
  **Why:** adversarial review proved the full-pass mechanism is not
  growth-monotone — a later connection could preempt an earlier blocked one, so
  a new scan moved existing nodes (6 of 364 replayed arrivals) and a
  loop-closing scan could re-parent an attached system, violating the ratified
  fixed-slot posture.
  **Authority:** Operator: adopted the replay correction in-session
  (2026-08-01) after plain-English review of both mechanisms.
- **Plan statement:** owned surfaces list five kernel modules; SC-5.4 expects
  exactly one engine "beside the contract, facts, proof-kit, and test files".
  **Built instead:** two additional single-purpose modules — `geometry.ts`
  (shared planar predicates + heading convention) and `overflow.ts` (orphan
  parking) — plus `determinism-fixture.ts` (committed digests) and a
  `siblingSpread` knob on `LayoutConfig`.
  **Why:** contract §11 complexity caps forced decomposition; the fan-width
  knob was ratified at the gate from rendered spread variants.
  **Authority:** Operator: G-1 posture ratification (spread); Evidence: Fallow
  complexity/duplication gates on the changed files.
- **Plan statement:** ELK probes run "through the promise API in Vitest".
  **Built instead:** uncommitted per-cell child processes with tree-killing
  timeouts.
  **Why:** the WEDGE_COMPACTION infinite loop cannot be interrupted in-process;
  a Vitest-hosted probe hangs the suite.
  **Authority:** Evidence: reproducible hang (corpus seed 12), observed live.
- **Plan statement:** DC-3's absolute stability wording, operator-relaxed by
  PD-3 to a measured, configurable axis.
  **Built instead:** the ratified `fixed-slot` posture meets the original
  absolute wording anyway (leaf adds, root-child adds, and sector fills move
  zero attached nodes), with one stated exception: parked-orphan clusters may
  shift internally when an orphan attaches or the cluster base steps a
  four-ring quantum outward.
  **Why:** adversarial review showed the cluster's slot compaction and quantum
  step were undisclosed movement; pinning orphans to sparse stable slots would
  scatter the parking area for a transient state.
  **Authority:** Operator: accepted the orphan-cluster exception (2026-08-01).

## Successor notes

- Async-bridge residual for 4.0.3.1.2: `reconcileChain` consults its assigner
  synchronously and `use-map-chain.ts` re-merges only on `chainSignature`
  change, so a kernel result resolving after a merge has no re-merge trigger —
  the worker bridge needs an explicit one plus defined
  first-appearance-before-resolution rendering, without changing the frozen
  reconciler/intent contract.
- Cross-engine float identity is open: within-engine determinism is pinned by
  the committed digest fixture, but `Math.sin`/`cos`/`hypot` are
  implementation-approximated per ECMA-262 and `segmentsIntersect` branches on
  exact orientation zeros, so different browser engines could diverge
  structurally. 4.0.3.1.2 must settle this before wiring the kernel to live
  clients (quantization, or computing layout in one place).
- Any change to `DEFAULT_LAYOUT_CONFIG` (the 4.0.3.1.2 dials) invalidates
  `determinism-fixture.ts`; regenerate deliberately. Standing dial invariant:
  `ringSpacing ≥ minSeparation`, documented on `LayoutConfig`.
- A future spawn-posture or root-override affordance must be synchronized map
  state, never local (HC-2); operator noted a per-map posture toggle as a
  future product option (2026-08-01).
- Camera-refit residual carried from 4.0.2.3.1: arrivals can land off-viewport;
  the integration session owns whether to bring them into view.
- `proportional` remains code-supported for live tuning; post-correction corpus
  aesthetics: 5 tree-tree crossings (vs 0 for fixed-slot) — re-tune before
  ratifying it as a default.
