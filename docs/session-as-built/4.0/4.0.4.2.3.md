## Delivered outcome

Atlas now renders every system as a widget frame, shows tracked pilots with
honest freshness in the frame and intelligence cards, points toward off-map
pilots, derives a deterministic bounded k-space halo, and paints world-anchored
fog that reveals authored truth without storing or authoring provisional
systems. The final 4.0.4.2 session also closes the hidden-tab tracking
validation: jumps continue behind the EVE client, the AFK gate pauses the feed,
and returning resumes it.

## Divergences from plan

- **Plan statement:** Pilot presence reads `feedFreshAt` from a read-only join inside `mapTracking.forMap` (PD-4 / Ordered work 2).
  **Built instead:** A sibling quantized `mapTracking.feedFreshness` query owns the per-owner-character covered-set join; presence also treats a fresh location `observedAt` as proof that the feed covered that character.
  **Why:** Joining the hot sync-subject stamp into `forMap` widened the automatic-jump observer's read set and broke its doorbell premise; the live tokenless engine can honestly write a location observation while its later covered-set stamp is empty.
  **Authority:** Evidence: close-out reliability and ownership review findings, accepted and fixed before delivery.
