## 1. Objective

Close Phase 4 and version 3.10: resolve the operator's live-use punch lists
within the fixed fix vocabulary so every correction lands in a primitive, tone
map, or registered token and propagates to all consumers; prove the phase's
universal claim by re-running the recorded adoption survey clean; and publish
the version's terminal release records in this sub-version's PR. It is its own
sub-version because punch-list discovery is unbounded and gated on a real soak
of the shipped refined site.

## 2. Current context and dependencies

- **DEP-1:** Sub-versions 3.10.4.1 and 3.10.4.2 are shipped to production; the punch list is reported by the operator from live use of that refined state, across multiple resumable sittings on this session's branch.
- **DEP-2:** The approved adoption survey's recorded detection methods (`docs/version-audits/3.10/PHASE_4_ADOPTION_SURVEY.md`) are the re-audit contract, with rails landed by the earlier bundles standing in for greps where they supersede them; its keep-as-is verdicts are adoption-scope only, so deliberate 3.10.4.2 restyling is not drift.
- **DEP-3:** The allowed fix vocabulary is fixed: existing primitives, feature tone-map files, and registered tokens — never a new one-off style; a material change outside it pauses for amendment or routes to backlog with citation.
- **DEP-4:** The seed planner checks are inherited without treating them as complete: cockpit KPI figures share one baseline whether or not tiles contain controls, ME/TE color meaning comes only from the industry tone map, and owned-blueprint highlighting is consistent across NodeCard, HeroCard, CockpitBuildPlan, and MeAdjuster.
- **DEP-5:** This sub-version's PR is the planned version-final PR: after its rows turn terminal the resolver proceeds to the version-close audit, which stays out of this session's scope.

and the version's delivery phase, and its PR is the planned version-final PR.
