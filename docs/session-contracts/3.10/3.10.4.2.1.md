Give the whole site the approved calmer hierarchy inside shared primitives and
tokens so every improvement propagates by construction — typography roles,
header restraint, page modes, deliberate surface hierarchy, and the layered
content rail — and make mobile an intentional, verified mode with a declared
breakpoint ladder. This is deliberate visual change, gated twice by the
operator, and it completes outcome group A by retiring the CSS families the
