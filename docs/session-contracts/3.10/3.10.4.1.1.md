## 1. Objective

Complete the first half of outcome group A: every bypass row the operator
dispositioned into this bundle is migrated onto its owning primitive or tone
map without changing the intended look, the primitive capability gaps that
forced those bypasses are closed as shared decisions, the superseded
page-scoped CSS families retire, and every migrated family becomes
mechanically detectable in the same PR. This bundle is its own sub-version
because its acceptance claim — nothing changed visually — is proven by capture
comparison and would be destroyed by combining it with deliberate restyling.
