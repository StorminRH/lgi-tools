## 1. Objective

Turn a classified wormhole jump into shared map fact, observed mass and a
privacy-safe verified observation with no user interaction.

## 2. Current context and dependencies

- **DEP-1:** Session 4.0.4.2.1 shipped tracking and pure jump classification.
- **DEP-2:** Sub-version 4.0.4.1 shipped gated authoring mutations and the mass model.
- **DEP-3:** The chain schema already includes signatures; real parser-created signatures arrive in 4.0.4.3.
- **DEP-4:** Decision D16 fixes origin-side attribution and forbids map, pilot and far-side untyped identity in observations.

## 3. Done conditions

- **DC-1:** A wormhole jump idempotently authors its destination and connection for every map viewer.
- **DC-2:** Gate travel and movement beyond a wormhole exit author nothing.
- **DC-3:** The crossed connection's mass estimate is decremented by the observed ship.
- **DC-4:** A jump-verified observation records the origin-side system and attributable type without map or pilot identity.
- **DC-5:** Exactly one eligible unresolved signature links automatically; ambiguity or no signatures links nothing.

## 4. In scope

- **IS-1:** Classified-jump authoring and repeated-arrival idempotency.
- **IS-2:** Observed mass decrement and D16 jump observation emission.
- **IS-3:** Hole matching against authoritative signature fixtures and its no-signature degradation.

## 5. Out of scope

- **OOS-1:** Fog and activity promotion, owned by 4.0.4.2.3.
- **OOS-2:** Signature parsing, lifecycle and inference, owned by 4.0.4.3.

## 6. Hard constraints

- **HC-1:** Only wormhole exits are authored.
- **HC-2:** Repeated arrival cannot duplicate a system or connection.
- **HC-3:** Observations contain no map or pilot identity, never attribute the far-side untyped code and never consume inferred values.
- **HC-4:** Hole matching links only one unambiguous eligible signature.

## 7. Decisions the session plan must resolve

- **PD-1:** Idempotency and concurrent-scout behavior.
- **PD-2:** Observation event shape and typed-side attribution across a jump.
- **PD-3:** The authoritative fixture/interface used to prove matching before the parser ships.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1:** A wormhole jump appears on a second client and repeated processing creates no duplicate — proving DC-1.
- **AC-2:** Gate travel and post-exit travel author nothing — proving DC-2 and HC-1.
- **AC-3:** The observed ship decrements the crossed connection estimate — proving DC-3.
- **AC-4:** Event inspection proves origin attribution and the absence of map/pilot/far-side identity — proving DC-4.
- **AC-5:** Single, ambiguous and absent-signature fixtures produce link, wait and no-op outcomes — proving DC-5.
