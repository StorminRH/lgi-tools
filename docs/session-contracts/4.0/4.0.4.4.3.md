## Session 4.0.4.4.3 — Archive and bounded purge

**Sub-version:** 4.0.4.4
**Master plan:** `docs/VERSION_4_0_PLAN.md` §4.0.4.4
**UX gate:** Yes
**Execution profile:** Frontier autonomous coding agent
**Delivery unit:** One agent session, one shared sub-version branch, one sub-version PR
**Roadmap coverage:** §4.0.4.4 archive, restore, grace period, resumable collaborative purge and durable tombstone
**Internal phases:** 1. Land archive and restore; 2. Land resumable bounded purge after grace; 3. Prove complete removal and review the lifecycle
**Split triggers:** Complete purge cannot respect platform operation limits without changing the shipped chain schema

## 1. Objective

Close a map safely through an archive-and-grace lifecycle with no path that
instantly destroys a living chain.

## 2. Current context and dependencies

- **DEP-1:** Sessions 4.0.4.4.1 and 4.0.4.4.2 shipped creation, switching and access management.
- **DEP-2:** Decision D3 keeps durable metadata in Neon and chain documents in Convex.
- **DEP-3:** Platform operation limits require bounded, resumable collaborative deletion.

## 3. Done conditions

- **DC-1:** Archiving immediately removes a map from switchers and remains owner-restorable during grace.
- **DC-2:** No instant hard-delete path exists for a living chain.
- **DC-3:** After grace, a resumable sweep purges every collaborative document in bounded batches.
- **DC-4:** Complete collaborative removal is followed by the durable tombstone.

## 4. In scope

- **IS-1:** Archive, restore and grace-period state.
- **IS-2:** Bounded purge, interruption recovery and durable tombstoning.

## 5. Out of scope

- **OOS-1:** Removing the development wall, which follows the version audit.
- **OOS-2:** Map settings beyond access and lifecycle management.

## 6. Hard constraints

- **HC-1:** Purge begins only after grace and no instant hard delete exists.
- **HC-2:** Batches respect current collaborative operation limits and resume safely.
- **HC-3:** The durable row is tombstoned only after collaborative deletion completes.

## 7. Decisions the session plan must resolve

- **PD-1:** Grace-period length.
- **PD-2:** Batch size, progress ownership and interruption recovery.
- **PD-3:** Proof that every map-owned entity kind participates in purge.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1:** Archive hides the map and restore returns it during grace — proving DC-1.
- **AC-2:** Inspection finds no immediate hard-delete path — proving DC-2 and HC-1.
- **AC-3:** A populated test map is removed completely across multiple resumable batches — proving DC-3 and HC-2.
- **AC-4:** The durable tombstone occurs only after complete collaborative removal — proving DC-4 and HC-3.

## 9. Verification

- **V-1:** Archive/restore browser demonstration.
- **V-2:** Interrupted and resumed multi-batch purge evidence.
- **V-3:** Complete entity census, operator review and the standing definition-of-done gate.

## 10. UX/operator gates

- **G-1:** The operator reviews archive and restore before the sub-version pull request opens.

## 11. Baseline/hotspot boundary

Expected baseline effect is Neutral. Planning must keep purge resumability in a
focused owner and avoid widening AF-006 or AF-007 query-export surfaces.

## 12. Close-out behavior

This is the final roadmap session for v4.0. It completes the shared branch,
pauses for operator review, and opens the sub-version pull request. After merge,
every roadmap row is terminal and the next lifecycle action is version-audit
planning; the development wall remains until that audit closes.
