## v4.3 — Clear Signal

v4.3 keeps your EVE data fresh for exactly as long as EVE says it is, and makes the whole site easier to read. Pages show your saved data straight away and update in place a few seconds later when newer data arrives. Names load faster, and during EVE's daily downtime the site stops asking EVE and keeps showing what you have.

Titles and labels across the site drop the all-capitals style for normal case. Atlas, sign-in and error messages use short, plain words, and when something fails you're told why and what to do next.

### v4.3.1 — 2026-10-13

This version changes how the site keeps EVE data fresh, closes an account security gap, and makes the whole site read plainer. A character you unlink can no longer sign its new owner into your account. Titles and labels use normal case, and Atlas, sign-in and error messages say what happened in plain words.

It also brings many small fixes across Atlas, industry, search and the home board.

#### Added
- The admin Health page lists the EVE requests that most often come back with an error. The admin ESI page shows that share on its rate-limit card, amber above 1% and red above 5%. It's for display only and doesn't send alerts.
- Admin notes on slow requests show how much of the time went to the main service it waited on, and how much wasn't timed.
- Every EVE permission the site asks for now has an explanation on the settings page.

#### Changed
- All EVE data, from prices and cost indices to skills, jobs, assets, blueprints, structures and corporation roles, now stays fresh exactly as long as EVE says it is. Pages show your saved copy straight away and update in place when newer data arrives.
- During EVE's daily downtime, once the site sees Tranquility is down it stops asking EVE until 11:30 UTC, and pages keep showing your saved data.
- Character, corporation and other EVE names load faster and in one go. A name that fails to load fills in on a retry about 30 seconds later, and an older saved name is shown when EVE can't answer.
- Titles and labels across the site use normal case instead of all capitals. Only the logo, share images, wormhole and signature class tags, and the system name on the map's summary card stay in capitals.
- Atlas text is shorter and plainer, with internal terms removed. For example, the no-access screen says "You don't have access to this map. Open another map, or ask a map admin for access."
- Error messages give the real reason in plain words: an ended session asks you to sign in again, a rate limit says how long to wait, and connection failures say to check your connection.
- A failed EVE sign-in returns you to the home page with a plain notice instead of an error code.
- Opening settings while signed out sends you home with "Log in with EVE to open your settings."
- Numbers read more consistently. Negative amounts and margins use a true minus sign, 999,960 ISK shows as 1.0M instead of 1000K, ages just under a month read "4w ago" instead of "0mo ago", and empty quantities show "—".
- The industry profile team table shows job slots as used over total, with "?" for an unknown half, matching the capacity panel and the home board.
- The planner refreshes a blueprint's prices in one request when you open it, and system cost indices refresh when viewed.
- Pages that ask EVE for data respond faster, and market prices and price history are fetched more at a time.
- The station list, system list and wormhole codex load once per page and retry every 15 seconds if they fail.
- The structure editor colours security like the rest of the site, shows "—" for unknown security, and says "Structure limit reached. Delete one to make room." when you're at the limit.
- The home-system dialog in Atlas shows tracking portraits only for characters the map allows, with name tooltips and arrow-key movement. Start tracking appears only when your character can be tracked there.
- In Friendlies, a pilot whose name hasn't loaded shows as "Character 1234" instead of a bare number.
- Board and admin charts can be read point by point with the arrow keys. Flat lines draw sensibly, and missing values show as gaps instead of zero.
- Admin, settings and the content browser share one side menu layout, and admin date ranges switch without reloading the page.
- Shared links to Industry Jobs and the Planner get their own preview title and description, and industry share cards use the site's standard preview image.
- Much of the site's code was rebuilt from shared building blocks over a long series of clean-ups.
- Tests were trimmed of low-value checks and folded into fewer, clearer tests, with shared test helpers.
- The site moved to newer versions of its web framework and other tools, picking up their security fixes.
- The site no longer crashes when a server setting is missing or empty.

#### Fixed
- A character you unlink from your account, for example before selling it, can no longer sign its new owner into your account. Characters join an account only through "Add character" while signed in.
- Deleted characters no longer come back when a new version of the site goes live.
- When an account is deleted, people it shared maps with can no longer get back into those maps. If the deletion is called off or fails, the owner's maps come back as deleted maps they can restore once they log in again.
- Unlinking or moving a character always finishes its clean-up, so map tracking and map access stay correct.
- Pilots and corporations with very large hangars or blueprint libraries can now sync them, and a failed refresh can no longer leave assets or structures empty.
- Corporation jobs, assets and structures keep syncing when one member loses the needed role, by trying the next member who has it. Role changes also count sooner.
- The limits of 50 custom structures and 20 industry profiles now hold when you save from several tabs at once.
- Creating two industry profiles at the same time no longer fails.
- If your characters fail to load on the home board, you now see "Your characters didn't load" with a retry button.
- The home board no longer shifts sideways when it finishes loading, and a logo that fails to load keeps its space.
- The header status panel shows a real EVE success rate, and the game data row turns amber only when a newer game build is out.
- Wormhole site details refresh after a game data update. Ore and gas site prices in search no longer switch to Atlas prices after you visit a map, and the site preview's neut value shows the right sign.
- In Atlas, connecting a system to itself now says "A system can't connect to itself." instead of closing as if it worked.
- In Atlas, a refused tracking change from the home-system dialog now shows "Tracking not changed", and a failed restore shows "Restore failed".
- Restoring a cut branch in Atlas brings back its open holes straight away.
- A connection made from a signature shows the correct lifetime, and duplicate wormhole codes no longer disagree.
- Revoke, block, delete and purge confirmations keep showing their target while closing, and a double click no longer sends the action twice.
- When someone loses access to a map, their other tracked pilots are removed from it too.
- Creating a map with a character that isn't linked gives a clear message, and the map's character search moves on to your next character when EVE refuses the first.
- Map access search keeps its results when you type a trailing space, and the access editor no longer closes when another map changes.
- Escape closes an open menu before the panel around it, and a few small flickers on the map and progress bars are gone.
- Menus and tooltips inside dialogs and side panels show on top again, "Try again" on error pages works, and label colours match across screens.
- Search picks a suggestion when you drag and release on it, and its Industry Jobs and Structures rows open their pages directly.
- Recent searches, recent blueprints and preferences keep working when browser storage is blocked or full.
- Bad or crafted links show a proper not-found page or notice instead of a server error, and a few forms that showed raw data now return you to the page with a notice.
- Reaction jobs are labelled Reaction instead of Industry, and unknown names read "Type 34" or "Character 12" without a "#".
- The planner's net margin no longer flashes too high on first load, and an outage at the backup price source no longer stops prices from refreshing.
- Screen readers hear one loading status per area instead of many, and checkboxes, switches and close buttons are named properly.
- The admin user page shows "Verification delayed" for a paused EVE check, and cancelled searches no longer count as outages on admin health.

#### Removed
- The background refresh queue and the admin Queue page. EVE data now refreshes when you view it.
