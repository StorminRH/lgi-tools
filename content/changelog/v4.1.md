## v4.1 — What is on the chain

4.1 keeps Atlas as the shared wormhole map you already use. Open a map, paste, jump, click a system. Once something is drawn, it stays put, and the chain shows more of what you already scanned.

### v4.1.7 — 2026-09-30

Atlas maps now decide tracking per character, not per account. A character can be tracked on a map when it is on the map's access list itself, by name or through its corporation. An alt outside a listed corporation needs to be added by name. When you create a map you pick at least one of your own characters to put on it, and admins can add or remove their own characters by portrait in Manage. Other pilots' account ids no longer reach your browser. Existing maps move over on their own: every character already tracked there is added to the access list, so nobody loses tracking.

#### Added
- Map creation asks which of your characters go on the map. At least one is required.
- Manage shows your own characters as portraits you can add to or remove from the access list.

#### Changed
- The tracking menu lists only your characters that are on the map's access list.
- Characters inside a listed corporation can be tracked, including alts. Alts in other corporations must be added by name.
- A character that leaves a listed corporation stops being tracked on that map. Your account keeps its access.
- Map edits and the event log are signed with one of your characters on that map: the first one you tracked there, else the first one added to the list. Older entries keep their names.
- Existing maps switch to per-character tracking during the daily maintenance run. Characters tracked there before the switch are added to the access list with your current role.

#### Fixed
- Tracking now refuses a character that is not on the map's access list, including characters that are not yours.
- Live tracking and coverage no longer send other pilots' account ids to the browser.
- The map creator can no longer be left with none of their own characters on the access list.

### v4.1.6 — 2026-09-28

Corporation data is now shared only when a Director turns sharing on. With sharing on, each member sees what their in-game roles let them see: the named stations, hangar divisions and containers they can view, deliveries, every blueprint for Factory Managers and every asset for Accountants. With sharing off, only Directors see the corporation's assets and blueprints, while Station Managers still see its structures and Factory Managers still see its industry jobs. LGI.tools now asks EVE for two more permissions, corporation divisions and member tracking, so every pilot needs to reconnect once.

#### Added
- Corporation items show where they sit in game: the station or structure name, the hangar division name and the container name.
- Members see only the corporation hangar divisions, deliveries and containers their in-game roles let them view, at headquarters, at their base and everywhere else.
- Two new EVE permissions, corporation divisions and member tracking. Every pilot reconnects once.

#### Changed
- One sharing switch per corporation now covers assets, blueprints and structures. Only Directors can turn it on or off in Corporation settings, and members can see whether it is on.
- Turning sharing off no longer deletes the corporation's structures, rig fits or facility taxes.
- Station Managers and Directors see and edit the corporation's structures whether sharing is on or off.
- Corporation roles are saved for up to an hour, so pages no longer ask EVE for them on every visit.

#### Fixed
- Corporation assets and blueprints are no longer shown to every member of the corporation.

#### Removed
- The Station Manager structure sharing switch. The Director switch replaces it.

### v4.1.5 — 2026-09-28

Adding a character that already belongs to another one of your accounts now joins the two accounts. The older account stays and everything from the other one moves onto it: characters, maps, preferences, saved plans, custom structures, net worth history and which pilots you track on each map. You stay signed in, now as the joined account. Where both accounts had a setting for the same thing, the older account's value is kept.

#### Changed
- Adding a character another of your accounts already holds joins the two accounts onto the older one, instead of moving that single character across.
- Which EVE account owns a character is recorded the first time it is linked, so a character that changes hands can never carry an LGI.tools account with it.
- Every kind of account data now says how it moves when two accounts join, and a check fails when one is missing.

#### Removed
- The "Character moved" notice on the Characters page. Joining is silent.

### v4.1.4 — 2026-09-27

Signed in, the home page is now your characters, in the spirit of EVEMon. The hero folds away and the board takes its place. With several pilots you see every pilot in a rail, main pilot first, each with what it is training, how long its queue has left and where it is, next to a wealth overview: your estimated net worth across all pilots and how it has moved over time. Pick a pilot and the board opens that pilot full width: skill queue, wallet and net worth, attributes and implants, clones, industry and skills. All characters, Escape or Back returns to the overview. With one pilot you go straight to its page. The Skill Queues page is gone; its queues live on the board. Pilots linked before this update need to reconnect once to share their wallet, clones, implants, structure names and market orders.

#### Added
- A pilot rail with portraits, online status, the skill in training, a queue line that turns amber under a day and red when the queue is paused or empty, the current system, and Add character at the end.
- Estimated net worth: your ISK plus the market value of what your pilots own, priced from recent Jita prices, with a chart of ISK and assets over time and each pilot's share. When net worth is far larger than your ISK, the chart splits its scale so both lines stay readable. The (?) beside it says what is and is not counted.
- Industry totals across pilots, with who has jobs ready to deliver.
- A full character page: corporation, alliance, security status, age, location and ship, then the skill queue, wallet and net worth with the recent journal, attributes and implants, clones, industry and every trained skill by group.
- A compact skill queue showing the next five skills, with the whole queue a click away in a drawer.
- Totals say how many pilots they cover when some are missing data, and one reconnect line per pilot names what a reconnect would add.
- Each pilot's page has its own address, so Back and Forward move between the overview and a pilot and a reload reopens it.

#### Changed
- Signed in, the character board takes the hero's place directly under the header. Signed out, the hero's search bar is replaced by the EVE sign-in button; search stays in the header.
- Skills that finished training count as trained straight away, even before EVE updates the pilot's skill list.
- /skills redirects to the home page.

#### Removed
- The home page feature cards, live stats strip and EVE news card.
- The Skill Queues page and its tool entry.

### v4.1.3 — 2026-09-24

Wormhole systems with an effect now glow in that effect's color on the chain, and known-space systems appear as small planets colored by their security. The system card and dock show the effect with its own icon under the statics.

#### Added
- A soft colored glow around wormhole systems with a Pulsar, Black Hole, Magnetar, Red Giant, Cataclysmic Variable, or Wolf-Rayet effect. Each effect pulses in its own rhythm while you hover or select the system.
- Known-space systems on the chain appear as planets tinted by their security color.
- The wormhole effect and its icon in the system card and dock.
- Each system's effect is read from the game's own static data.

### v4.1.2 — 2026-09-24

The chain shows which systems hold identified harvestables, hacking, or combat. Colored icons sit around each system, and the shared system card expands into sites, statics, trade-hub distances, and friendly pilots with their ships.

#### Added
- Colored ring icons for harvestables, hacking, combat, and friendlies.
- Expandable identified site totals, with live harvestable estimates and catalogue combat blue-loot totals. Unknown estimates show a dash.
- Wormhole statics with type codes, destination classes, and the original CCP wormhole icon.
- All five trade hubs, closest first, on known-space system cards.
- Region and the nearest reachable trade hub above known-space nodes.
- Expandable friendly counts with pilot names and ships or Docked.

#### Changed
- Identified site summaries take priority over raw signature and anomaly counts.
- Connections stop and fade before node icons and captions, with pilot arrows aligned to the visible connection.

#### Fixed
- Connection strokes recover after overlapping nodes separate.
- Repeated edge IDs across maps no longer share another connection's gradient.

### v4.1.1 — 2026-09-07

A wormhole system's statics now sit on the map as real holes from the moment that system appears. Once a hole or system is drawn, paste, identify, jump, and delete leave it where it is. You can no longer unlock the map to drag systems around, and the Auto layout setting is gone. Camera follow and click focus stay.

Atlas tracking now shows when a character needs to reconnect and brings you back to the same map after sign-in. Ambiguous jumps wait for your signature choice before adding the destination. Open tabs share tracking work, and failed jump and signature requests can recover.

#### Added
- Reconnect on characters missing location access, with an orange portrait ring and a return to the same map after sign-in.

#### Changed
- A system's static wormholes appear on the map as soon as that system does.
- Identifying a scanned signature as the static fills the hole that was already drawn, instead of making a new one.
- Pasting another wormhole, jumping a hole, or deleting a signature leaves every hole and system that was already drawn where it is.
- Opening the same map in another browser shows the same picture.
- Atlas loads signatures for the system you are viewing and pauses detailed log updates while the log is closed.
- Open Atlas tabs share routine tracking updates and recent jump results.
- Repeating an unchanged signature paste or wormhole type selection skips work that already succeeded.
- Character tracking avoids database work when there is nothing to sync.
- Industry planner batches share one material calculation.
- Search history updates consistently, and leaving lightbox mode closes the sites card.
- Shared data lookups, dependency updates, and internal review tools reduce repeated work and keep the app maintained.

#### Fixed
- A character missing location access no longer looks the same as an offline character.
- Ambiguous jumps wait for a signature choice before the destination appears, including when a static hole is identified while the choice is pending.
- Jumping back before answering counts mass once and leaves the unresolved system off the map.
- Recording or answering an ambiguous jump closes undo on a dying hole between the same systems.
- Failed signature and wormhole type requests retry on the next identical input, and abandoned jump requests become retryable.
- Older tracking requests cannot overwrite or clear newer login information.
- Stopping tracking clears saved jump bookkeeping after the last tracker leaves, while other users' active tracking continues.
- Adding a system on staging draws its static holes again.
- The server-status indicator keeps its loading appearance until the page is ready.

#### Removed
- The Auto layout setting.
- Unlocking the map to drag systems around on your own screen.
- Unused analytics writes, character-field usage, and redundant tests.
