## v4.2 — Codex

4.2 opens Codex, a field guide to wormhole space inside LGI.tools. Pilots write the advice, and the game data stays live.

### v4.2.0 — 2026-10-04

Codex is open to everyone. Every wormhole type, site, wormhole class, and guide has its own page that pairs live game data with advice written by pilots. Any signed-in pilot can suggest an edit, the site admin reviews it, and suggestions that touch different parts of a page merge on their own. Pages can carry screenshots, YouTube and Twitch videos, and live game data. Site search finds Codex pages, and each site's full page now lives in Codex.

#### Added
- A Codex home with a tile for each kind of page and its count, a title search, and the latest edits.
- Every wormhole type, site, and wormhole class has its own page with its live game data and sections ready to write.
- Guides with a breadcrumb, sections, and an "On this page" list. They can hold headings, lists, quotes, tip boxes, tables, and links.
- Screenshots in any page: paste or drop an image into the editor and add alt text, and readers get a sharp copy sized for their screen. A suggested image waits for review like the text.
- YouTube and Twitch videos in any page: paste a link and give it a title, and readers see a thumbnail that loads the player only when they press play.
- Live game data for a wormhole type, a site, a wormhole class, or any item, shown as an info box, a table, a value inside a sentence, or the site card. The numbers update when the game data does.
- Any signed-in pilot can suggest an edit and follow its review on a page of their own suggestions. Accepted suggestions credit the pilot at the bottom of the page.
- A suggestion still goes in after the page changed. Edits to different parts merge on their own, and the site admin settles any part both sides changed by seeing the original, the page now, and the suggestion side by side.
- The site admin can start a guide, edit one section or a whole page in place, and restore an earlier version from the page history. Readers never download the editor.
- Every page keeps its full edit history, and its text is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Codex in the main menu on desktop and mobile.
- Site search finds Codex guides, wormhole types, and wormhole classes.
- Codex pages are listed for search engines, and a shared site page shows a preview card.

#### Changed
- Each site's full page now lives in Codex. Site cards on Wormhole Sites have an "Open in Codex" link, site search results open the Codex page, and old site links forward there.
- An opened site card on Wormhole Sites stays open after a reload.

#### Removed
- The old standalone site page and its strip of related sites.
