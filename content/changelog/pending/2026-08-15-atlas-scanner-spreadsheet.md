#### Changed
- The Atlas scanner is now a docked spreadsheet. Type, mass, lifetime, and destination edit in place, and an unknown signature can be identified as a typed wormhole without opening the editor.
- Setting or clearing a destination keeps the hole and retargets it, or returns it to an unresolved stub, instead of requiring a sever.

#### Fixed
- Ordinary Perimeter Deposit is recognized when the name is typed with the common Permiter misspelling.
